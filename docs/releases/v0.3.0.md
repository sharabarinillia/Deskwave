## What's in Deskwave 0.3.0

The first public release.

- **Animated wallpapers.** You can use:
  - Aurora, a built-in animation;
  - your own videos, GIFs and images;
  - gradients you make yourself: linear, radial or flow, still or moving.

  Each display can have its own wallpaper.
- **Desktop widgets:** clock, notes, to-do, focus timer, battery, system stats, now playing and
  weather. Move, resize, add and remove them right on the desktop.
- **Private:**
  - offline by default;
  - no account and no telemetry;
  - when you turn the internet on, only the widgets you allowed can use it, and only for the
    sites they list.
- **Light on your PC.** Animations pause during games, full-screen video, with maximized windows,
  on battery and while the PC is locked.
- **Seven languages:** English, Українська, Français, Nederlands, Deutsch, Polski, Español.
- **Mods.** Anyone can write widgets in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. They run in a sandbox and stay
  off until you approve them.
