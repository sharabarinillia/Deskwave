**Animated wallpapers and desktop widgets for Windows and macOS.** Private, offline by default, free for non-commercial use.

> [!WARNING]
> **Only download Deskwave from this page.** Copies from anywhere else may have been changed and are not safe.

## Download

Pick a file under **Assets** at the bottom of this page. Not the "Source code" files: those
are the project's code, not the app.

| File | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| **`Deskwave-Setup.exe`** | **Windows, most people.** Installs for your account only. No administrator rights needed. |
| `Deskwave.msi` | Windows, IT admins and managed PCs. Installs for all users. Needs administrator rights. |
| `Deskwave-Windows.zip` | Both Windows installers in clearly named folders, with a READ ME FIRST file. |
| **`Deskwave-macOS.dmg`** | **Macs** with macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Fingerprints to check that your download is genuine. |

**Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?** Deskwave isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
If you downloaded it from this page: select **More info**, check that the app name starts with
**Deskwave**, then select **Run anyway**.

**macOS says it can't check Deskwave?** Deskwave isn't notarized by Apple yet. If you downloaded
it from this page:
- macOS 15 or later: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and select
  **Open Anyway**;
- macOS 12 to 14: Control-click Deskwave in Applications and choose **Open**.

Never turn off your antivirus or Gatekeeper to install Deskwave.
[More about these warnings](https://github.com/sharabarinillia/Deskwave#install).

## What's new in 0.4.0

- **Deskwave for Mac.** One app for Apple Silicon and Intel:
  - wallpapers and widgets on every display and every Space;
  - a menu bar icon;
  - your accent color and languages.

  Now playing stays Windows only, because macOS doesn't let apps see what other apps are playing.
- **Keep awake (Mac, off by default).** For local AI agents and other work that runs overnight:
  - keep the screen on, or only the Mac;
  - turn it on under General or from the menu bar icon;
  - while it's on, animations pause after 10 minutes without use.
- **Albums.** Videos and photos take turns as your wallpaper:
  - one at a time, or as a collage of up to four;
  - photos stay up from 5 seconds to an hour, and videos play to the end;
  - in your order, or shuffled.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag videos and photos into the Settings window to add them, or a mod's
  folder onto the Mods page. "Open folder" still works too.
